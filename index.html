About the studio

Designing Spaces
With Purpose.

Ruang is an interior design studio based in Bali. We help homeowners, villa owners, and business owners create spaces that are not only beautiful — but functional, comfortable, and true to the people who use them.

From a compact apartment in Denpasar to a private villa in Canggu or a café in Seminyak, every project begins with listening. We study how you live and work, then translate it into layout, material, light, and detail.
